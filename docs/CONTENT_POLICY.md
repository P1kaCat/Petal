# Publication and reporting policy

Authors must own their submissions or have explicit permission to host and
distribute them. Submission requires a distribution declaration. Petal's code
license does not replace an author's content license or grant permission to mirror
files from Modrinth, CurseForge or other providers.

## Review and revisions

Uploaded releases stay private until reviewed. Published bytes are immutable:
updates require a new release. Authors and maintainers can withdraw a submitted
or published release; public download and discovery access then stop immediately.
Private retention and cleanup follow the storage policy.

Title, description, license, source link, icon and gallery changes become pending
project revisions. The previous approved content stays public until approval.
Rejection leaves it unchanged. Reviewers provide a reason; decisions are recorded
and audited. A pending revision based on older project content cannot overwrite a
newer approval: it must be resubmitted against the current revision. A decided
revision cannot be decided twice.

Descriptions are plain text and escaped on every web view. Active HTML/SVG is not
accepted as an image. PNG, JPEG and WebP uploads are decoded with a 16-million-pixel
limit, stripped of source metadata and re-encoded as static WebP up to 1600px.
Images have a 5 MB input limit and remain private until referenced by an approved
project with a published release. Galleries have at most ten images. These
validation rules do not prove copyright ownership or mod safety.

## Teams

An owner can invite contributors or maintainers. Invitees must accept before
membership takes effect. Contributors can inspect team submissions and review
history; maintainers can submit releases and revisions. Only the owner can change
membership or transfer ownership. Transfers require an accepted recipient, revoke
the previous owner's ownership and create an audit record. A maintainer cannot
grant itself ownership.

## Reports and private notes

Signed-in accounts can report a published project. Reports remain visible to
their submitter and the moderation team, with a maximum of 20 open reports per
account. Reviewers record a private resolution. Other authors and public visitors
cannot inspect reports by guessing their identifiers. Release review notes and
revision decision reasons are available to authorized teams and reviewers;
public version responses omit private review notes.

Moderation considers distribution permission, project licensing, compatibility
declarations and file contents. Archive validation does not perform a malware
scan. Public deployment additionally requires the scanning or explicit manual
review policy documented by the storage/deployment configuration.
